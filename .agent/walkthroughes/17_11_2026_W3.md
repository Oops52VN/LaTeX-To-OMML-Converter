## 1. Objectives Completed

1. **Fixed Mixed English + Raw LaTeX Conversion (`Mixed_English_LaTeX_Formulas.docx`)**:
   - **Root Cause**:
     - When paragraphs lacked `$` or `\[`/`\(` delimiters, `core/src/tokenizer/scanner.rs` evaluated `is_pure_latex_formula(input)` across the whole paragraph (`MATH_INDICATORS` substring match or `< 30` whitespace tokens with `^`/`_`), classifying entire English+LaTeX sentences (~50% of `Mixed_English_LaTeX_Formulas.docx`) as a single monolithic `<m:oMathPara>` equation.
     - Additionally, `core/src/docx_engine/xml_stream.rs` wrote `<m:oMathPara>` unconditionally from `omml_map` even when formulas were embedded inline alongside `<w:r>` prose runs.
   - **Fix**:
     - Replaced whole-paragraph heuristic classification in `core/src/tokenizer/scanner.rs` with a UTF-8-safe 3-pass structural tokenizer (`lex_raw_tokens` -> `bind_math_atoms` -> bidirectional `scan_raw_latex_segments`), isolating raw LaTeX formulas from surrounding English/natural-language prose and trailing punctuation while preserving pure standalone formulas (`10000_Formulas.docx`) and avoiding false positives on URLs, Windows paths, code blocks, contractions, and `snake_case`.
     - Updated `core/src/docx_engine/xml_stream.rs` (`write_omml_for_context`) and `app/app.py` (`process_paragraph_xml`) to emit inline `<m:oMath>` whenever a formula shares a paragraph with non-whitespace text, keeping `<m:oMathPara>` strictly for standalone display equations.
     - Recompiled the Rust extension (`cargo build --release`) and updated both `core/python/quast_core/_quast_core.pyd` and `site-packages/quast_core/_quast_core.pyd`.

2. **Fixed Multi-File Preview Overwrites & Cache Inefficiency**:
   - **Root Cause**:
     - On a cache hit in `WebMainWindow.preview_file`, `current_preview_req_id` and `word_engine.active_req_id` were never invalidated, allowing an in-flight background render (e.g., an output file) to emit `on_preview_ready` and overwrite a subsequently selected cached file (e.g., an input file).
     - `PreviewCacheManager._memory_cache` keyed only by `norm_path` (ignoring `mtime_ns` and `size`) and never cached Stage 1 `_p3.pdf` slices; interrupting Stage 2 left no cache entry, forcing full re-renders on re-selection.
     - `PersistentWordPdfEngine` allowed heavy Stage 2 exports (tens of seconds on 10k-formula files) to starve subsequent Stage 1 previews.
     - `resources/UI/UI.html` unconditionally called `showPreviewLoader(true, ...)` in `setFileList` `div.onclick`, flashing the loading overlay and hiding `EdgePdfPreviewHost` on every click even for cached files, while `EdgePdfPreviewHost.load_pdf` lacked a navigation sequence guard on its 150ms fallback timer.
   - **Fix**:
     - Upgraded `PreviewCacheManager` (`app/app.py`) to a two-tier `(norm_path, sha256_key)` cache covering both full `.pdf` and 3-page `_p3.pdf` partial previews.
     - Refactored `PersistentWordPdfEngine` (`app/app.py`) into a 3-tier queue (`_active_stage1_queue`, `_bg_stage1_queue`, `_bg_stage2_queue` with 1.0s Stage 2 cooldown and active-file priority) and added `clear_active_request()`.
     - Added strict `docx_path == current_preview_file` checks in `WebMainWindow.on_preview_ready`, `_batch_gen` timer guards, full `cache_manager.clear()` in `Backend.reload()`, `_nav_seq` + `_should_be_visible` guards in `EdgePdfPreviewHost` (`app/edge_pdf_viewer.py`), and removed the premature click loader in `resources/UI/UI.html`.
